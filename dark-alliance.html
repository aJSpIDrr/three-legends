The game

Name your lord. Raise your banner.

Dark Alliance is an online RPG set in a gothic world of plague roads, drowned keeps and burning saints. You start as an Outcast in the Ashen Mire and fight your way up to the Throne of Night.

Every mission pays gold and experience. Every battle is won by the lords who stand behind you, so you build an alliance, arm it, and lead it into battle against rival houses. Buy land for steady income, hide your gold in the vault, and hunt down the lords with a price on their heads.

How it plays

Five places to go

Everything in the game sits under five tabs: Home, Battle, Shop, Allies and Hero.

  • Home

    The Realm shows your lord, live events and everything happening around you. Missions and Updates sit right beside it.

  • Missions

    Eight chapters with five missions each. Missions cost energy and pay XP and gold. Master each one three times over, then face the chapter boss to open the road ahead.

  • Battle

    Fight rival lords for their gold, hunt marked lords on the Bounty board, and join the realm against the World Boss, the Invasion and the Tourney.

  • Shop

    Items, Relics and Equipment for every lord you lead, land and buildings for your Empire, blessings at the Altar, and the Vault to keep your gold safe.

  • Allies

    Found or join a clan, chat live, fill the clan treasury, upgrade the Training Grounds and take on Clan Sieges together. Climb the Ranking with your Alliance.

  • Hero

    Your lord, your stats and your sigil. Spend your points and shape how you fight.

  • World events

    World Bosses arrive on a timer, Invasions and Tourneys run live, and Clan Sieges pit your whole clan against titans like Ymrak the Frost Titan.

  • Empire income

    Your lands pay gold every hour. Keep upkeep below income, collect often, and bank what you can before a rival takes it.
