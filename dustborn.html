The game

The old world is buried. You were born on top of it.

Dustborn is an online RPG set in a burned-out wasteland. You lead a crew of scavengers, take jobs for caps and salvage, and fight your way through fourteen regions, each one guarded by a boss.

Other players are out there too. Raid their camps for caps, put a price on their heads, or team up to bring down the roaming hordes. Build outposts that earn while you sleep, and turn salvage into guns and war rigs at the Workbench.

How it plays

Life in the wastes

  • Jobs

    Jobs cost energy and pay caps, XP and salvage. Each has its odds; if it goes wrong you lose a little health. Run a job 3 or 10 times in one go, master it for a salvage reward, and clear a whole region for a big payout.

  • Your crew

    Everyone in your crew carries one of your guns and one of your rides, best first. Hire drifters or bring friends to grow.

  • Raids

    From level 4, spend ammo to raid other crews. Your attack against their defense, plus a little luck, decides it. Winners take part of the loser's unprotected caps.

  • Wanted wall

    Pay to post a crew on the wall. Anyone who knocks them out claims the reward, whatever their level.

  • Roaming hordes

    Hordes show up for a few hours and only fight crews in their level range. Top damage dealers and whoever lands the final blow earn scrip.

  • Outposts

    Outposts earn every hour, even while you are offline, until they fill up. Upgrade your safe to protect more of your caps.

  • Workshop & Bazaar

    Buy gear from the Trader, build stronger gear at the Workbench from region blueprints, or buy rare tech with scrip.

  • Every day

    Spin the Scav Wheel, check in to grow your streak, and finish two daily jobs for scrip.
