The game

Start on the street. End up running it.

Syndicate City is our next online RPG. Pick one of six crime factions from around the world, work jobs across the city, build your family and fight rival bosses for turf.

Every rank changes your portrait, your frame and your perks, all the way from a nobody in a rainy alley to the boss of the whole city. This page is a small sneak peek. There's a lot more we're keeping under wraps for now.

Pick your side

Six factions. Three revealed.

Each faction has its own city, its own mentor, ten ranks to climb and a signature weapon nobody else can carry.

The Five Families

New York

Old-school mafia. Pinstripe suits, speakeasies and loyalty above all. Strong on defence and cheaper crew.

The Firm

London

East End gangsters with a talent for cleaning dirty money. Banking costs them almost nothing.

The Syndicate

Johannesburg

The Jozi Gold Crew: gold and diamond smuggling and armoured cash-in-transit heists. Big job cash and tough on the road.

What you'll be doing

  • Work the city

    Run jobs across eight districts, master each one, then take down the district boss for gold and a one-off trophy.

  • Build your family

    Recruit a crew, found a crime family with its own crest and go to war with rival families for turf.

  • Your own HQ

    Grow your headquarters from a back room into a fortress, with a vault, a casino and a helipad on the roof.

  • Forge your weapon

    Every faction has a signature weapon. Upgrade it from Mk I all the way to Mk X.
